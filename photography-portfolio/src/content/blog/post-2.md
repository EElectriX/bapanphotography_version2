# A Milestone That Will Always Stay Special

Receiving recognition for your work is always an incredible feeling, but this award was something I never imagined.

Our photograph **"The South Indian Bride"** was awarded **2nd Place** in **Category 42 – Non-Manipulated Photography** at the **American Art Awards**. Knowing that our work was appreciated on an international platform means more than words can express.

What makes this achievement even more special is that the competition featured entries from **64 countries**, and our photograph represented **India**, securing **Second Place**. It is a moment of immense pride for me and for everyone who was part of this journey.

This image had previously received a nomination in a photography competition in **West Bengal**, but I never expected it to earn such recognition on the global stage. It reminds me that sincere work and dedication can travel much farther than we often imagine.

This achievement would not have been possible without the incredible people who stood beside me:

- **Makeup Artist:** Riya Halder
- **Model:** Sangeeta Geet
- My entire team, whose hard work made this vision a reality.
- My friends and supporters, who have continuously encouraged, motivated, and believed in my work.

To everyone who has been a part of this journey—**thank you**. Your support inspires me to keep creating and pushing my craft further.
